Privacy policy

SnapMatch: Pose Camera · Effective 10 November 2026

SnapMatch does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Camera

The camera feed is analysed on your iPhone to find body positions and line you up with a pose. Frames are never uploaded or saved unless you take a photo.

Photos

Photos you choose as a pose, and pose grids you import, are copied into the app's private storage on your iPhone so you can reuse them. Finished photos are saved to your Photos library only when you tap Save. Nothing leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. SnapMatch has no servers that receive your data. If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

Children

SnapMatch does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete imported poses in the pose library (touch and hold, then Delete), or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
