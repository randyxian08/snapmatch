SnapMatch: Pose Camera for iPhone

SnapMatch puts a see-through pose over your camera, shows you where to move, and takes the photo when you line up.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: randyxian08@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

The camera is black. Open Settings › SnapMatch and turn on Camera.

It never shoots by itself. Make sure auto-shoot (the wand button) is on, and that the whole person is in frame. Hold still for half a second once the dots turn dark red.

How do I use a pose grid I saved? Open the pose library, tap Import and pick the image. SnapMatch splits the grid into single poses.

How do I delete an imported pose? In the library, touch and hold the pose, then tap Delete.
